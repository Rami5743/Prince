Prince of Persia — the potion room

The same engine, the same renderer and the same sound as the game next door. The only difference is one level record: a corridor with all six of the game's potions a step apart, because the real game has one feather potion, at the bottom of a shaft in level 7, and two upside-down ones, thirteen levels in.

Walk up to a flask and press Shift to drink it. That is the original's own way in: control_standing is if (control_shift2 == CONTROL_HELD && control_shift == CONTROL_HELD && check_get_item()) return;, and control_shift2 is a latch, so the key has to go down again rather than be held. Ctrl+A lays them all out afresh.

The corridor runs left to right, two rooms, a floor tile between each:

  1. blue — hurt. One hit point, sound_13, no flash. It is first so that the one behind it has a flask to fill.
  2. small red — heal. One hit point back, sound_33, and the screen blinks red twice. With a full flask it does nothing at all, which is the C and not a fault.
  3. big red — life. add_life: a new flask, sound_30, red four times.
  4. green — feather. feather_fall(): green three times, sound_39, and he falls at a pixel a tick instead of three for as long as that tune is in the one channel — eighteen and a half seconds, measured off a capture of PRINCE.EXE. Nothing else sounds while it lasts, which is the game: 39 is marked uninterruptible at priority nought.
  5. upside-down. toggle_upside() and nothing else: no flash, no noise. Press it again to come back.
  6. the one that opens the gates. trigger_button(0, 0, -1), no flash and no noise. There are no gates in this corridor, so it is here to be heard not to be seen.

Everything else the game does works here as well — the cheats, the clock, the pause. Shift+W is the feather without the flask, and Shift+I turns the screen over without one.
