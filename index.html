Prince of Persia, Reconstructed

The 1990 DOS game, rebuilt to run here — reading the original's own data files and the tables inside its executable.

Arrow keys to move, shift to step carefully, and hold up to jump. Pick a level from the menu; pick a room to look around without playing.

Standing, turning, running, jumping, climbing, hanging and falling are in, and so are the traps: gates and the buttons that work them, loose floors, spikes and chompers. Guards and sound are not yet. Every room here has been compared against the real game running under emulation, and matches it pixel for pixel.
